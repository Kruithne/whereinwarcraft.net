Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 13/08/26

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Game scores you submit
  • Communications you send to us (if any)

Battle.net Login

You can log in with a Battle.net account. Blizzard Entertainment operates Battle.net. We use the Battle.net login only to identify you on the leaderboards.

You enter your Battle.net details on the Blizzard website. The privacy policy of Blizzard Entertainment covers that step. We never receive your password. Blizzard sends us two items:

  • Your Battle.net account identifier
  • Your BattleTag (for example, Kruithne#2222)

We do not request your email address. We do not request access to your game data or your characters.

We store the account identifier, the BattleTag, your display name, the date you first logged in, and the date you last logged in. The display name is the BattleTag without the number (for example, Kruithne).

Information Automatically Collected

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Game usage statistics and gameplay data
  • Technical information about how you use our website

Third-Party Advertising

Our website displays advertisements served by Mediavine, Inc. ("Mediavine"). Mediavine may collect and use information about your visits to this and other websites to provide advertisements about goods and services that may be of interest to you. This information may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser information
  • Device identifiers
  • Browsing behavior and interests
  • Demographic information

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the web game
  • Identify you when you log in
  • Display submitted scores
  • Improve the game experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

Public Leaderboards

When you submit a score, we show these items on a public leaderboard page:

  • Your display name
  • Your score
  • Your accuracy

We show the display name only. We never show the number part of your BattleTag. We never send the full BattleTag to your browser or to our public interfaces.

We keep one record for each game mode. A new score replaces your record only if the new score is better.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties, except:

  • With Mediavine for advertising purposes as described above
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With service providers who help us operate the website (under confidentiality agreements)
  • On the public leaderboards, as described above

We do not send your information to Blizzard Entertainment. Blizzard receives your login request directly, because you log in on their website.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to display game scores and as required by applicable law. More exactly:

  • We keep your account record and your leaderboard scores until you ask us to delete them.
  • We delete a login session one year after you last use it.
  • We delete the game data for a game when you submit the score.
  • We delete game data five days after your last guess, if you do not submit the score.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access to your personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of your information
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Objecting to processing

For EU residents: You have additional rights under GDPR. You may withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

You can log out at any time. A logout ends your session but keeps your leaderboard scores. To delete your account, your display name and your scores, contact us.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies:

  • Essential cookies for website functionality
  • Analytics cookies (via third-party advertising partners)
  • Advertising cookies (via Mediavine)

We set these essential cookies:

  • session_id — keeps you logged in. It expires after one year.
  • battletag — shows your name in the site header. It expires after one year.
  • pending_score — holds a score while you log in to submit it. It expires after 15 minutes.

We also keep your current game progress in the local storage of your browser. This data stays on your device. You can clear it with your browser tools.

You can control cookie settings through your browser, but some features may not work properly if you disable cookies.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages.
